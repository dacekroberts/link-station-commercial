# Tone guide: Dace Roberts

For any session helping with Dace's writing, in any repo. Self-contained: it
needs no other file.

**Suggest; don't rewrite.** Every line a session proposes is a draft until
Dace approves it.

Status: first version, 2026-10-11. Built from three of Dace's academic
essays and the prose Dace wrote for the light rail site. To be revised after
the first round of story reviews.

## Voice to keep

- First person, with a stated position: "I chose", "I'd argue", "I left out".
- Concede first, then disagree. Give the other reading its fair sentence.
- One small concrete case per idea ("Say a café is choosing between...").
- Define a term the first time it appears, in a clause.
- Plain idioms are welcome. Repeat a key term; don't swap in synonyms.
- End on the honest limit: what this doesn't show.

## What changes for a business or general reader

The academic habits stay; the order and the size change.

- **Answer first.** The thesis moves from the end of the first paragraph to
  its first sentence. The wide opener shrinks to one line or goes.
- **Shorter units.** About 15 to 25 words a sentence, one idea each; 3 to 5
  sentences a paragraph.
- **Headings do the signposting on a page.** Keep spoken transitions ("With
  that out of the way...") for the talk track.
- **One formal connector per paragraph at most.** "So" and "but" do most of
  the work; "Thus" and "Hence" are for method sections.

## Levels of communication

| Level | Length | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 1 sentence | the problem and the answer |
| Story | 30 to 60 seconds | problem, what was built, one or two results, one limit |
| Method | 2 to 3 minutes | sources, the key judgment call, how it was checked |
| Depth | only when asked | decisions log, audits, code |

A line belongs at the level where the reader would ask for it. If it answers
a question they haven't asked yet, move it down a level or cut it.

## Supporting data

- A number replaces an intensifier. Not "drastically lower": "62.9% lower".
- One figure per sentence, with its unit and what it counts.
- Exact where the figure is the point (r = 0.684); rounded when spoken
  ("about 63 percent").
- Hedge with the specific limit, not a general "seemingly": "licences active
  on one date, so closures don't show".
- Associations, not causes. No "significant" without a test.
- A figure that isn't Dace's carries its credit line.
